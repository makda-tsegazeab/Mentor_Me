MentorMe Suspension and Reports Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-01-20

Purpose
This policy defines how reports are handled and how suspension decisions are made.
It aims to protect user safety, preserve platform integrity, and ensure fair, consistent decisions.

Scope
Applies to all users (students, parents, tutors, admins) and all in-app interactions.

Definitions
- Report: A user-submitted complaint about another user or content.
- Suspension: Temporary or permanent restriction of account access.
- Severity: Level of enforcement based on risk, intent, frequency, and evidence.
- Code: A standardized reason category for enforcement.

Report Intake and Handling
1) Report channels
   - In-app report flows.
   - Admin-initiated review.
2) Required report fields
   - Reporter ID, reported user ID, report code, description, timestamp.
   - Evidence attachments if available (screenshots, messages, logs).
3) Acknowledgement
   - Report acknowledged in the system with a reference ID.
4) Triage
   - Safety-related reports get priority review.
   - Clear spam/bot evidence may be actioned immediately.

Suspension Codes
- SAFETY_HARASSMENT: Threats, harassment, stalking, or safety risk.
- FRAUD_IMPERSONATION: Identity misuse, impersonation, forged credentials.
- PAYMENT_ABUSE: Chargebacks, fee evasion, or payment manipulation.
- SPAM_BOT: Spam, automation, scraping, or bulk unsolicited messages.
- NO_SHOWS: Repeated no-shows without notice or agreement.
- POLICY_MINOR: Low-impact policy violations.

Severity Rubric
Score each dimension from 0 to 2. Total score is 0 to 8.
- Harm risk: none/low (0), moderate (1), high/credible threat (2)
- Intent: accidental (0), negligent (1), deliberate (2)
- Frequency: first time (0), repeated (1), ongoing/pattern (2)
- Evidence strength: weak (0), moderate (1), strong/verified (2)

Severity Mapping
- 0-2: Warning (no suspension)
- 3-5: Temporary suspension (24-72 hours)
- 6-7: Extended suspension (7-30 days)
- 8: Permanent suspension or review hold

Enforcement Actions
1) Warning
   - Inform user of policy breach and required corrective action.
   - Record in user history.
2) Temporary suspension
   - Duration: 24-72 hours.
   - Used for moderate severity or unclear intent with strong evidence.
3) Extended suspension
   - Duration: 7-30 days.
   - Used for repeated violations or high-impact behavior.
4) Permanent suspension
   - Used for severe fraud, safety threats, or repeated high-severity behavior.
   - Account remains blocked unless reinstated by admin review.

Mandatory Escalation Rules
- Any credible safety threat triggers immediate suspension and review.
- Verified fraud or impersonation triggers extended or permanent suspension.
- Multiple no-shows in a short period triggers at least temporary suspension.

Evidence Guidelines
- Strong evidence includes direct message logs, payment records, or verified identity proof.
- Moderate evidence includes multiple consistent reports.
- Weak evidence includes uncorroborated single reports.
- When evidence is weak, apply temporary suspension with investigation.

Appeals Process
1) Users may submit an appeal with supporting details.
2) Appeals are reviewed by an admin not involved in the original decision.
3) Outcomes: uphold, reduce, or lift the suspension.
4) Appeal decisions are logged in audit history.

Data and Audit Trail
Suspension actions store:
- suspended, suspendedAt, suspensionCode, suspensionScore, suspensionLevel,
  suspensionNote, suspensionRubric, adminId, createdAt.
Audit records are written to user_suspensions.

Reinstatement
- Automatic if the suspension duration ends (if tracked).
- Manual via admin action.
- Reinstatement should include a short note for audit completeness.

User Communication
- Users are informed of the suspension code and high-level reason.
- Sensitive evidence is not shared if it risks reporter safety.

Periodic Review
- Policy should be reviewed quarterly.
- Rubric thresholds may be adjusted based on observed outcomes.
